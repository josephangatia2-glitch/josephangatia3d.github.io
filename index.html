About Me

Animator. Rigger.
3D Generalist.

I'm Joseph Matende Angatia, a freelance 3D generalist and animator based in Nairobi, Kenya. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Film and Animation from Multimedia University of Kenya (2025), specializing in animation and character rigging.


My work spans character rigging, architectural visualization, AOV compositing, and rendering. I bring technical precision and creative vision to every project — whether it's a fully rigged character or a photorealistic interior.
